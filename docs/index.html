Belief

Six mechanisms, six demonstrations, and a map of how a story mutated as it was shared. Each demonstration asks you something first and only then shows a result — because the only way the lesson lands is if the mechanism works on you, once, in front of you.

Open Belief
Status Built and working offline: six demonstrations (anchoring, availability, confirmation bias, illusory truth, escalation of commitment, propaganda technique), each refused until its last step is answered; notes filed by chapter or under “unsorted”; progress that marks an unplayed demonstration as unplayed; and a rumor map that diffs versions against each other.
Not built: a packaged Windows EXE / Android APK release (see the README), and any population statistic — this app shows your answers, not other people's.

What it does

  • Puts you inside each mechanism: your own estimate, your own picks, your own ratings, your own path through a scenario.
  • Shows the result computed from what you typed, quoting the answer it used and naming the mechanism.
  • Marks a demonstration you have not played as unplayed — never as complete.
  • Files a note under the mechanism it belongs to; a note with no chapter goes to “unsorted” and says so.
  • Maps a story's mutation: paste versions with a source and a date, and see what appeared, what disappeared, which hedges were added and which numbers were dropped.

What it will not do

  • Tell you what to believe, or mock anyone who believes something. It explains the mechanism and lets you recognise it.
  • Invent evidence. There is no “70% of people…” anywhere: the only numbers are yours, and the app says so under each result.
  • Decide which version of a story is true. The map shows movement; truth needs the primary source.
  • Show a result before you answer. Every demonstration refuses until its last step is in.
  • Upload anything. No network at runtime; CSP connect-src 'none'.

When things go wrong

CaseWhat happens
Bad inputA note with no chapter is filed under “unsorted” and says so. A story version with no source or no date is refused, because a mutation with no provenance cannot be placed on a map.
No networkNothing changes: the demonstrations, the notes and the map are on the device.
Storage full or unavailableProgress and notes are not written and the app says the session will not be remembered; reading and playing continue.
Corrupt saved stateUnreadable progress is reported and offered for export, with an explicit “start from the beginning” — never a silent reset. An unreadable map is never rebuilt from a partial read, because a wrong chain is worse than no chain.

How to run it

  • Here: play/index.html, or install it from your browser's menu.
  • Offline file: open docs/play/index.html straight from disk.
  • Tests: node tests/self-tests.js — the spec's countable “done means” is asserted there, one demonstration at a time.
