same words as 12/05 · kept, marked, not merged
Two sources, one wording. The second forward is not a second source — it is the same sentence arriving twice, and the app keeps both so you can see that.

Kept, not merged

A repeat is marked and kept. Merging two identical versions would hide the fact that they travelled separately — which is the whole question.

What it refuses

It will not decide what is true. It shows you the versions, who said them and when, and which are the same words.
